Our service

Every scheduled visit includes

  • Deep interior cleaning
  • Grate cleaning
  • Grease tray cleaning
  • Burner surface cleaning
  • Heat plate cleaning
  • Firebox cleaning
  • Exterior stainless detailing
  • Removal of grease and carbon buildup
  • Operational function check (ignition and burner operation only)

Every visit also delivers

  • Before & after photographs
  • Digital service report
  • Maintenance observations
  • Recommended replacement parts, when visible wear is noted
Resident Ready Grills provides cleaning and preventive maintenance services only. We do not perform or certify gas, plumbing, electrical, fire safety, or code inspections.
